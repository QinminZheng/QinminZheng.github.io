2027 CSC scholarship recruitment

Fully Funded Master's and PhD Opportunities at Beihang University

The Computational Physics & Data Science Research Group led by Prof. Qinmin Zheng welcomes outstanding international students to apply for the 2027 Chinese Government Scholarship High-level Postgraduate Program at Beihang University.

Research opportunities

Successful applicants will pursue research at the intersection of fluid mechanics, high-fidelity computation and data-driven modelling. Current directions include:

  • Computational fluid dynamics, turbulence physics and high-speed compressible flows.
  • Deep learning, generative models and scientific machine learning for fluid mechanics.
  • Data assimilation, flow-field reconstruction and uncertainty quantification.

Applicants with backgrounds in mechanics, aerospace, mechanical engineering, energy and power, mathematics, automation, artificial intelligence or computer science are encouraged to contact us.

Scholarship and programme

Funding
Tuition, university accommodation or accommodation subsidy, stipend, and comprehensive medical insurance. International travel is not covered.
Master's
Two to three years; applicants must hold a bachelor's degree and be under 35.
PhD
Four years; applicants must hold a master's degree and be under 40.
Eligibility
Applicants must be non-Chinese citizens, be in good physical and mental health, and meet Beihang's academic admission requirements.
English
For English-taught programmes: IELTS Academic 6.0, TOEFL 90 or Duolingo 105, obtained within two years.
Registration
September 2027

Scholarship quotas are limited and applications are reviewed in batches. Prospective students should contact Prof. Zheng and complete their applications as early as possible.

How to express interest

Email zhengqinmin@buaa.edu.cn with:

  • Your CV, academic transcripts and degree information.
  • A brief statement of research interests and whether you are applying for the Master's or PhD programme.
  • Evidence of English proficiency and representative publications, projects or code, if available.

Suitable candidates may then discuss supervision, the required acceptance letter and the supervisor-signed study plan. An initial email does not replace the formal university and CSC applications.

Official application requirements

  • Apply through both the Beihang International Student Online Application System and the CSC system.
  • Select CSC programme category Type B and Beihang University agency number 10006.
  • Meet the applicable academic, health and language requirements and submit all supporting documents.
