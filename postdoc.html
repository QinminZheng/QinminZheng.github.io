Postdoctoral recruitment

Postdoctoral Positions in Deep Learning, Data Assimilation and Turbulence

The Computational Physics & Data Science Research Group at the Hangzhou International Innovation Institute, Beihang University invites applications for two full-time postdoctoral positions.

Position details

Appointment
Full-time for two years
Salary
RMB 200,000–320,000 per year, depending on the candidate's expertise
Openings
2 positions
Teaching
No teaching duties

Desired profile

  1. You have defended your doctoral thesis within three years of the date of the job offer.
  2. You hold a Ph.D. in computational fluid dynamics, deep learning, data assimilation or a related field.
  3. You are proficient in Fortran, C++ or Python.
  4. You have experience in high-fidelity computation and turbulence analysis, or in applying deep-learning or data-assimilation methods to fluid mechanics or aerodynamics.

What we offer

  • A friendly, international and equitable working environment.
  • Supplementary health insurance with healthcare coverage in addition to social security.
  • Flexible working hours and arrangements.

How to apply

Please email the following documents to zhengqinmin@buaa.edu.cn:

  • A cover letter describing your interest in and suitability for the position.
  • A full CV including your publication list.
